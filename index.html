    About this directory

    This directory lists current smart home hardware from major manufacturers: lighting control, climate, security, access, networking, audio and video. Each product has its part numbers, a description and the public prices we found, with a link to where each price came from.

    How prices work

    • Only public prices are included: list, MSRP, retail and street prices. Installer and trade pricing is not.
    • "From" is the lowest price we found in any region, converted at reference exchange rates. VAT is removed where the source price included it, so prices compare like for like.
    • The detail panel shows each price in its original currency and region, with the date it was checked. Prices change, so confirm with a supplier before quoting.
    • Many high-end systems sell only through installers. Those products are listed without a price.
